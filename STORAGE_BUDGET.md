# STORAGE_BUDGET.md

Hard limit: 40 GB
Soft target: 30-35 GB

Suggested:
- Website/search/content: <1 GB
- Images/diagrams: 5-10 GB
- Offline manuals/docs: 5-8 GB
- Tools/installers/portable packages: 15-20 GB
- Samples/practice binaries: 2-5 GB
- Headroom: >=5 GB

Do not sacrifice article quality to save text space.
Deduplicate large binaries and images first.
